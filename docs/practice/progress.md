# My Progress

Your status for every study page and your flashcard results, grouped the way the site is. Change a page's status here or in the study bar under its title; both stay in sync.

<div id="progress-dashboard"><p>Loading your progress…</p></div>

## What the statuses mean

| Status | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **Not started** | You haven't read the page yet |
| **Learning** | You've read it, but you can't yet answer every interview question on it without peeking |
| **Confident** | You can explain the page's topics in your own words, with the trade-offs, and answer its questions out loud |

The home page's *Continue your path* card points to the first page marked **Learning**, or to the first **Not started** page if nothing is in progress.

## Where your progress is stored

Everything is saved in this browser's local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there is no account.

- **Export progress** downloads a small JSON file with your page statuses and flashcard marks.
- **Import progress** loads such a file, for example on a second laptop or after clearing your browser data. Imported values overwrite matching entries and leave the rest alone.
- **Reset all progress** erases every status and flashcard mark in this browser. It asks you to click twice.

!!! warning "Private windows and cleared data"
    A private or incognito window forgets everything when it closes, and clearing site data erases your progress. Export a copy now and then if you rely on it.
