# Flashcard Drill

Every interview question on every page of this site is in this deck, so new questions show up here on their own. Pick a section or a single page, answer **out loud** before you reveal the answer, then mark it honestly.

<div id="drill" class="drill"><p>Loading questions…</p></div>

## How the drill works

| You do | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Show answer** (<kbd>Space</kbd>) | Reveals the full answer from the topic page, with math and tables |
| **Review again** (<kbd>1</kbd>) | The question is marked weak and comes back about twice as often as an unseen one |
| **I knew it** (<kbd>2</kbd>) | The question is marked known and comes back rarely, so you still revisit it |
| **Skip** (<kbd>3</kbd>) | Moves on without changing anything |

- With *Ask unseen and "review again" questions more often* switched on, the drill weights its picks: review again ×6, unseen ×3, known ×1. Switch it off for uniform random picks.
- The link above each card opens the topic page, where the question sits in context with diagrams and the questions around it.
- Your marks are saved in this browser only. To move them to another browser or device, use **Export** and **Import** on [My Progress](progress.md).

!!! tip "A 15-minute daily routine"
    1. Five minutes on **All sections** to keep everything warm.
    2. Ten minutes on the section you're studying this week.
    3. In the last week before an interview, drill only the sections the role emphasizes; the [study plans](../start/study-plans.md#tracks-by-role) list them by role.

!!! interview "Answer the way you would in the room"
    Mark **I knew it** only when your spoken answer covered the core idea, the mechanism, and at least one trade-off or failure mode. Recognizing the answer when you see it doesn't count: interviews test recall, not recognition.
