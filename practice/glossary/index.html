    Glossary¶

    Short definitions of the terms used on this site. Each links to the page that explains it in context. Abbreviations also show their expansion when you hover over them anywhere on the site.

    A · B · C · D · E · F · G · H · I · K · L · M · N · O · P · Q · R · S · T · U · V · W · X–Z

    A¶

    A/B test
    A randomized experiment that compares a control and a treatment on live traffic, sized in advance for a minimum detectable effect. Evaluation
    A2A (Agent2Agent)
    An open protocol for agents to discover each other through agent cards and delegate tasks, with streamed status updates and artifacts. It complements MCP, which connects an agent to tools and data. MCP & A2A
    Activation function
    The non-linearity after a layer (ReLU, GELU, SiLU, tanh, sigmoid). Without it, stacked layers collapse into one linear map. Neural networks
    Adam, AdamW
    An adaptive optimizer that combines momentum with per-parameter step sizes and bias correction. AdamW applies weight decay separately from the adaptive step, and is the default for Transformers. Training
    Advantage
    How much better an action is than the policy's average in that state, \(A = Q - V\). Policy-gradient methods weight updates by it. RL essentials
    Agent
    An LLM running in a loop that chooses its own next action, calls tools, observes the results, and stops when the goal is met. Contrast with a fixed workflow. AI agents
    Agent harness
    Everything around the model in an agent: the loop, tools, sandbox, permissions, context management, verification, state, tracing and evals. Harness engineering
    ALiBi
    A position method that adds a distance-proportional penalty to attention scores instead of position embeddings, which helps with longer inputs than seen in training. Transformer
    Approximate nearest neighbor (ANN) search
    Index structures such as HNSW, IVF and product quantization that find close vectors quickly by giving up exactness. Embeddings
    Attention
    A weighted average of value vectors, with weights from the similarity of a query to each key. It lets every token read directly from every other token. Transformer
    AUC (ROC-AUC)
    The area under the ROC curve: the probability that a random positive is scored above a random negative. Evaluation
    Autoencoder
    A network trained to reconstruct its input through a narrow bottleneck, which forces a compressed representation. Generative models
    Autoregressive model
    A model that generates one token at a time, each conditioned on everything before it. GPT-style LLMs are autoregressive. Pretraining

    B¶

    Backpropagation
    Reverse-mode automatic differentiation: the chain rule applied backward through the network, giving the gradient for every parameter in one pass. Neural networks
    Bagging
    Training models on bootstrap samples of the data and averaging them to reduce variance. Random forests are bagged trees. Trees & ensembles
    Batch normalization
    Normalizing activations with the mean and variance of the current batch, per channel, then rescaling. It speeds up and stabilizes CNN training. Training
    Beam search
    Decoding that keeps the \(B\) most likely partial sequences at each step. Good for translation; bland for open-ended chat. Pretraining
    BERT
    An encoder-only Transformer pretrained by predicting masked tokens from both sides. Strong for classification, extraction and embeddings. Pretraining
    Bias–variance trade-off
    Expected error splits into bias squared (the model is too simple), variance (it's too sensitive to the training sample) and irreducible noise. ML fundamentals
    BM25
    A keyword ranking function built from term frequency (with saturation), inverse document frequency and document-length normalization. RAG
    Boosting
    Adding weak learners one at a time, each correcting the errors of the ensemble so far, which reduces bias. Trees & ensembles
    Bradley–Terry model
    Models the probability that response A is preferred over B as \(\sigma(r_A - r_B)\). It's the loss used to train reward models on preference pairs. Post-training
    Byte-pair encoding (BPE)
    A tokenizer that starts from bytes or characters and repeatedly merges the most frequent adjacent pair into a new token. Transformer

    C¶

    Calibration
    Whether predicted probabilities match observed frequencies: of all cases predicted at 0.8, about 80% should be positive. Fixed with Platt scaling, isotonic regression or temperature scaling. Evaluation
    Catastrophic forgetting
    Losing earlier capabilities when fine-tuning on new data. Reduced with lower learning rates, mixing in general data, or PEFT. Fine-tuning
    Chain-of-thought (CoT)
    Having a model write intermediate reasoning steps before its answer, through prompting or training. Reasoning
    Chinchilla scaling
    The finding that compute-optimal training scales parameters and data together, about 20 tokens per parameter. Pretraining
    Chunking
    Splitting documents into retrievable pieces for RAG, ideally along their structure and with some overlap. RAG
    Classifier-free guidance
    Steering a diffusion model toward its condition by extrapolating from the unconditional prediction toward the conditional one. Generative models
    CLIP
    Image and text encoders trained contrastively so matching pairs embed close together, which enables zero-shot image classification. CNNs & vision
    Cold start
    Recommending for new users or new items that have no interaction history. Recommender systems
    Collaborative filtering
    Recommending from the interaction patterns of similar users or similar items, without item content. Recommender systems
    Compaction
    Summarizing an agent's history when the context window fills, keeping decisions and open issues and dropping raw tool output. Context engineering
    Concept drift
    A change in \(P(y \mid x)\): the same inputs now lead to different outcomes. MLOps
    Constitutional AI
    Alignment that uses a written set of principles, with the model critiquing and revising its own outputs and AI feedback replacing much human labeling (RLAIF). Post-training
    Context engineering
    Choosing what enters the model's context window at each step (instructions, tools, retrieved data, memory, history, tool results) to get the most signal per token. Context engineering
    Context rot
    The drop in a model's recall and reasoning quality as its context window fills with more tokens. Context engineering
    Continuous batching
    Adding and removing requests from the running batch at every decode step, so the GPU stays busy. Inference
    Contrastive learning
    Learning embeddings by pulling matching pairs together and pushing non-matching pairs apart, for example with the InfoNCE loss. Embeddings
    Convolution
    Sliding a small learned filter over the input. It gives locality, weight sharing and translation equivariance. CNNs & vision
    Covariate shift
    A change in the input distribution \(P(x)\) while \(P(y \mid x)\) stays the same. MLOps
    Cross-encoder
    A model that reads the query and a document together to score relevance. Accurate but too slow for first-stage retrieval, so it's used for reranking. Embeddings
    Cross-entropy
    The negative log of the probability given to the correct class, averaged over examples: the negative log-likelihood for classification. Math for ML
    Cross-validation
    Estimating generalization by training and validating on several different splits; for time series, always train on the past and validate on the future. ML fundamentals
    Curse of dimensionality
    In high dimensions data becomes sparse and distances between points become similar, so nearest neighbors carry less information. ML fundamentals

    D¶

    Data leakage
    Information unavailable at prediction time leaking into training, such as future data or preprocessing fitted on the full dataset. It inflates offline results. ML fundamentals
    DBSCAN
    Density-based clustering that finds clusters of any shape and labels points in sparse regions as noise. Unsupervised
    Decision tree
    A model that splits data on feature thresholds, choosing each split to reduce impurity the most. Trees & ensembles
    Decoder-only Transformer
    A stack of causal self-attention blocks trained to predict the next token: the architecture of GPT-style LLMs. Transformer
    Diffusion model
    A generative model that learns to reverse a gradual noising process, and generates by denoising step by step from pure noise. Generative models
    Distillation
    Training a smaller student model to match a larger teacher's outputs or reasoning traces. Fine-tuning
    DPO (Direct Preference Optimization)
    Aligning a model on preference pairs with a classification-style loss, with no separate reward model and no RL sampling. Post-training
    Dropout
    Randomly zeroing activations during training so the network can't rely on any single unit; switched off at inference. Training

    E¶

    Early stopping
    Stopping training when the validation metric stops improving, a simple and effective regularizer. Training
    ELBO
    The evidence lower bound a VAE maximizes: reconstruction quality minus the KL divergence of the latent distribution from the prior. Generative models
    Embedding
    A dense vector representation in which similar items are close together. Embeddings
    Encoder–decoder
    An architecture in which an encoder reads the input and a decoder generates the output while attending to it, as in the original Transformer and T5. Sequence models
    Ensemble
    Combining several models' predictions to beat any single one, by bagging, boosting or stacking. Trees & ensembles
    Evals
    Test suites for LLM systems: a dataset of cases, graders (code, model or human) and metrics tracked across versions. Evaluating LLM systems
    Exploding gradients
    Gradients that grow exponentially through layers or time steps. Fixed with gradient clipping, careful initialization and normalization. Neural networks

    F¶

    F1 score
    The harmonic mean of precision and recall, a single number for an imbalanced positive class. Evaluation
    Feature store
    A system that serves the same feature definitions for training (point-in-time correct) and for online inference, preventing training–serving skew. MLOps
    Few-shot prompting
    Putting a few worked examples in the prompt to show the task and output format. Prompting
    Fine-tuning
    Continuing to train a pretrained model on task- or domain-specific data. Fine-tuning
    FlashAttention
    Exact attention computed in on-chip memory tiles with an online softmax, so the \(n \times n\) score matrix is never written to GPU memory. Efficient attention
    Flow matching
    Training a model to follow a velocity field that carries noise to data along near-straight paths, which allows fewer sampling steps than classic diffusion. Generative models
    FSDP, ZeRO
    Sharding optimizer state, gradients and weights across data-parallel GPUs, so models too large for one GPU can be trained. Fine-tuning
    Function calling
    See tool calling.

    G¶

    GAN
    A generator and a discriminator trained against each other. Produces sharp samples, but training is unstable and prone to mode collapse. Generative models
    GELU, SiLU
    Smooth, ReLU-like activation functions used in Transformers. Neural networks
    Gradient boosting
    Boosting in which each new tree fits the negative gradient of the loss; XGBoost, LightGBM and CatBoost are implementations. Trees & ensembles
    Gradient checkpointing
    Saving memory by storing only some activations and recomputing the rest during the backward pass. Fine-tuning
    Gradient clipping
    Rescaling gradients whose norm exceeds a threshold, preventing a single huge update. Training
    Gradient descent
    Repeatedly moving parameters a small step against the gradient of the loss. Math for ML
    Grounding
    Basing an answer on supplied sources, with citations, rather than on the model's memorized knowledge. RAG
    GQA (grouped-query attention)
    Groups of query heads share one key/value head, which shrinks the KV cache with little quality loss. Efficient attention
    Greedy decoding
    Always choosing the most likely next token. Deterministic but prone to repetition. Pretraining
    GRPO
    Group Relative Policy Optimization: PPO-style RL that samples a group of answers per prompt and uses each answer's reward, normalized within the group, as its advantage, with no critic model. Reasoning
    Guardrails
    Checks around an LLM (input and output classifiers, validators, policy filters, permission limits) that block or repair unsafe or invalid behavior. Security

    H¶

    Hallucination
    Fluent, confident output that is false or not supported by the provided sources. Pretraining
    Hinge loss
    The SVM loss \(\max(0, 1 - y f(x))\): zero for points beyond the margin on the correct side. kNN, NB & SVM
    HNSW
    A layered proximity-graph index for approximate nearest-neighbor search: high recall at low latency, but memory-heavy. Embeddings
    Human in the loop
    Requiring a person to approve high-impact actions, or to review predictions the system is unsure about. AI agents
    Hybrid search
    Combining keyword (BM25) and dense vector retrieval, usually merged with reciprocal rank fusion. RAG
    HyDE
    Hypothetical document embeddings: the LLM writes a plausible answer, and that answer's embedding is used to search. RAG
    Hyperparameter
    A setting chosen before training, such as learning rate, depth or regularization strength, tuned on validation data. ML fundamentals

    I¶

    In-context learning
    Performing a new task from instructions and examples in the prompt, with no weight updates. Prompting
    Inductive bias
    The assumptions built into a model's design, such as locality and weight sharing in CNNs. CNNs & vision
    Instruction tuning
    Supervised fine-tuning on instruction–response pairs so a base model follows instructions. Post-training
    IVF index
    An inverted-file index that clusters vectors and searches only the clusters nearest the query. Embeddings

    K¶

    k-means
    Clustering that alternates between assigning points to their nearest centroid and moving each centroid to the mean of its points. Unsupervised
    k-nearest neighbors (kNN)
    Predicting from the labels of the \(k\) closest training points. kNN, NB & SVM
    Kernel trick
    Computing dot products in a high-dimensional feature space through a kernel function, without ever building the features. kNN, NB & SVM
    KL divergence
    An asymmetric measure of how one probability distribution differs from another. Used as a penalty in RLHF and inside the VAE objective. Math for ML
    KV cache
    The stored keys and values of past tokens, so each decode step computes attention only for the new token. Efficient attention

    L¶

    L1 and L2 regularization
    Penalizing the absolute size (L1, which zeroes weights) or squared size (L2, which shrinks them) of weights to reduce overfitting. ML fundamentals
    Label smoothing
    Training on softened targets, such as 0.9 for the true class, to reduce overconfidence. Training
    Layer normalization
    Normalizing each token's features to zero mean and unit variance, independent of the batch; the standard in Transformers, often as RMSNorm. Training
    Learning-rate schedule
    Changing the learning rate during training, typically a short warmup followed by cosine or linear decay. Training
    LLM-as-judge
    Grading outputs with a language model and a rubric. Scales well, but has position, length and self-preference biases, so calibrate it against human labels. Evaluating LLM systems
    Logistic regression
    A linear score passed through a sigmoid and trained with log loss, for classification. Linear models
    Logit
    An unnormalized score before a sigmoid or softmax; for logistic regression, the log-odds. Linear models
    LoRA
    Fine-tuning by learning a low-rank update \(\Delta W = BA\) on frozen weights; the update can be merged in, so serving has no added latency. Fine-tuning
    LSTM
    A recurrent network with gated memory cells that update additively, preserving gradients over long sequences. Sequence models

    M¶

    Mamba
    A selective state-space model: a linear recurrence whose parameters depend on the input, giving linear time and constant memory per token. Efficient attention
    MAP estimation
    Choosing parameters that maximize likelihood times prior; regularization is a prior in disguise. Math for ML
    Masked language modeling
    Predicting randomly hidden tokens from the context on both sides: BERT's pretraining objective. Pretraining
    MCP (Model Context Protocol)
    An open protocol through which applications connect models to tools, resources and prompts exposed by servers, turning N × M integrations into N + M. MCP & A2A
    Memory (agents)
    What an agent keeps beyond the current context window: summaries, notes and to-do files, or a store of facts it searches when relevant. AI agents
    Mixed precision
    Computing in 16-bit floats while keeping 32-bit master weights, for speed and memory savings. Fine-tuning
    Mixture of experts (MoE)
    Layers with many expert networks and a router that sends each token to a few of them, growing parameters without growing compute per token. Efficient attention
    MLA (multi-head latent attention)
    Compressing keys and values into a small latent vector per token, shrinking the KV cache well below GQA's. Used in DeepSeek-V2 and V3. Efficient attention
    MLE (maximum likelihood estimation)
    Choosing the parameters that make the observed data most probable. Math for ML
    MLOps
    Practices and tools for getting models into production and keeping them working: pipelines, registries, deployment, monitoring and retraining. MLOps
    Model registry
    A versioned store of trained models with their metrics, lineage and approval status. MLOps
    MQA (multi-query attention)
    All query heads share a single key/value head: the smallest KV cache, with some quality loss. Efficient attention
    Multi-head attention
    Several attention heads in parallel, each with its own projections, so the model can track several relationships at once. Transformer
    Multimodal model (VLM)
    A model that takes images (or audio) and text, usually a vision encoder connected to an LLM through a projection. Generative models

    N¶

    Naive Bayes
    A classifier that applies Bayes' rule assuming features are independent given the class. kNN, NB & SVM
    NDCG
    A ranking metric with graded relevance and a logarithmic discount by position, normalized by the ideal ordering. Evaluation
    Next-token prediction
    The pretraining objective of GPT-style models: maximize the probability of each token given all the tokens before it. Pretraining

    O¶

    Out-of-bag error
    A random forest's built-in validation estimate, computed on the rows each tree didn't see in its bootstrap sample. Trees & ensembles
    Overfitting
    Fitting noise in the training data: training error keeps falling while validation error rises. ML fundamentals

    P¶

    PagedAttention
    Storing the KV cache in fixed-size blocks, like virtual-memory pages, to avoid fragmentation and share common prefixes; introduced by vLLM. Inference
    PCA
    Projecting data onto the orthogonal directions of greatest variance: the top eigenvectors of the covariance matrix. Unsupervised
    PEFT
    Parameter-efficient fine-tuning: training a small set of added or selected parameters, such as LoRA adapters, instead of all weights. Fine-tuning
    Perplexity
    The exponential of the average per-token cross-entropy; lower means the model finds the text less surprising. Pretraining
    Policy gradient
    RL methods that move a policy's parameters along \(\nabla \log \pi(a \mid s)\), weighted by how good the action turned out. RL essentials
    Position bias
    Users click higher-ranked items more regardless of relevance, so logged clicks overstate what was shown first. ML system design
    Positional encoding
    Order information added to attention, which is otherwise order-blind: sinusoidal, learned, RoPE or ALiBi. Transformer
    PPO
    Proximal Policy Optimization: policy-gradient RL with a clipped objective that keeps each update close to the previous policy. RL essentials
    Precision and recall
    Precision: the share of flagged items that are truly positive. Recall: the share of true positives that were flagged. Evaluation
    Prefill and decode
    Prefill processes the whole prompt in parallel (compute-bound, sets time to first token); decode generates one token at a time (memory-bound, sets time per token). Inference
    Prefix (prompt) caching
    Reusing the KV cache of a repeated prompt prefix across requests, cutting latency and cost. Keep stable content at the start of the prompt. Inference
    Pretraining
    Self-supervised training on huge amounts of unlabeled data before any task-specific training. Pretraining
    PRM and ORM
    A process reward model scores each reasoning step; an outcome reward model scores only the final answer. Reasoning
    Product quantization (PQ)
    Compressing vectors by splitting each into parts and storing a short codebook index for every part. Embeddings
    Prompt engineering
    Designing instructions, examples, structure and output formats so a model behaves reliably. Prompting
    Prompt injection
    Instructions hidden in user input or in content the model reads (web pages, documents, tool results) that hijack its behavior. Security

    Q¶

    QLoRA
    LoRA training on top of a frozen base model quantized to 4 bits (NF4). Fine-tuning
    Quantization
    Storing weights, and sometimes activations, in fewer bits (8 or 4) for cheaper, faster serving. Fine-tuning
    Query, key, value
    The three projections of each token in attention: what it's looking for, what it offers to match, and what it passes on. Transformer

    R¶

    RAG (retrieval-augmented generation)
    Retrieving relevant documents at query time and putting them in the prompt, so the model answers from them and can cite them. RAG
    Random forest
    Bagged decision trees that also consider a random subset of features at each split, to de-correlate the trees. Trees & ensembles
    ReAct
    An agent pattern that interleaves reasoning, actions (tool calls) and observations in a loop. AI agents
    Reasoning model
    An LLM trained, usually with reinforcement learning, to think at length before answering, trading inference compute for accuracy. Reasoning
    Recall@k
    The share of all relevant items that appear in the top \(k\) results; it caps what a RAG generator can use. Evaluation
    Reciprocal rank fusion (RRF)
    Merging ranked lists by summing \(1/(k + \text{rank})\) for each item across lists, usually with \(k = 60\). RAG
    Red teaming
    Deliberately attacking a system with adversarial inputs to find failures before users or attackers do. Security
    Regularization
    Anything that discourages fitting noise: weight penalties, dropout, early stopping, data augmentation. ML fundamentals
    Reinforcement learning (RL)
    Learning a policy that maximizes reward by interacting with an environment. RL essentials
    Reranker
    A slower, more accurate model, often a cross-encoder, that reorders the top retrieved candidates. RAG
    Residual connection
    Adding a block's input to its output, \(x + F(x)\), so gradients flow directly and the identity is easy to learn. CNNs & vision
    Reward hacking
    A policy exploiting flaws in its reward signal to score well without the intended behavior, such as longer answers or special-cased tests. Post-training
    Reward model
    A model trained on human preference pairs to score responses; the optimization target in RLHF. Post-training
    RLHF
    Reinforcement learning from human feedback: supervised fine-tuning, then a reward model trained on preferences, then RL (classically PPO) with a KL penalty. Post-training
    RLVR
    Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards: training on tasks whose results can be checked automatically, such as math answers or code tests. Reasoning
    RNN
    A network that processes a sequence one step at a time, carrying a hidden state forward. Sequence models
    ROC curve
    True-positive rate plotted against false-positive rate across all thresholds. Evaluation
    RoPE
    Rotary position embedding: rotates query and key vectors by an angle that depends on position, so attention scores depend on relative distance. Transformer

    S¶

    Sandbox
    An isolated environment where an agent's code and commands run, with limited file, network and credential access. Harness engineering
    Scaling laws
    Power-law relationships between a model's loss and its parameters, training data and compute. Pretraining
    Self-attention
    Attention in which queries, keys and values all come from the same sequence. Transformer
    Self-consistency
    Sampling several reasoning paths and taking the most common final answer. Reasoning
    Self-supervised learning
    Learning from labels derived from the data itself, such as the next token or a hidden image patch. Pretraining
    SFT (supervised fine-tuning)
    Fine-tuning on curated input–output demonstrations; the first post-training stage. Post-training
    Shadow deployment
    Running a new model on live traffic without acting on its predictions, to compare it with the current model safely. MLOps
    SHAP
    Feature attributions based on Shapley values from game theory, with consistency guarantees. Responsible AI
    Softmax
    Turns a vector of logits into probabilities: \(e^{z_i} / \sum_j e^{z_j}\). Neural networks
    Sparse vs dense retrieval
    Matching keywords over sparse term vectors (BM25) versus comparing learned dense embeddings. Embeddings
    Speculative decoding
    A small draft model proposes several tokens and the large model checks them in one pass, keeping the large model's output distribution exactly. Inference
    State-space model (SSM)
    A sequence model built on a linear recurrence that trains in parallel and runs with constant memory per token, such as S4 and Mamba. Efficient attention
    Stochastic gradient descent (SGD)
    Gradient descent that estimates the gradient from a random mini-batch at each step. Training
    Structured outputs
    Constraining generation to a JSON schema or grammar so the output always parses. Prompting
    Sub-agent
    A separate agent with its own clean context that handles one subtask and returns a condensed result to the main agent. Context engineering
    Support vector machine (SVM)
    A classifier that maximizes the margin between classes; kernels make it non-linear. kNN, NB & SVM
    System prompt
    The instructions an application gives the model to set its role, rules, tools and context for a conversation. Prompting

    T¶

    t-SNE and UMAP
    Non-linear methods that map high-dimensional data to 2D or 3D for visualization, preserving local neighborhoods; distances between clusters aren't meaningful. Unsupervised
    Teacher forcing
    Training a sequence model on the true previous tokens rather than its own predictions. Sequence models
    Temperature
    Dividing logits by \(T\) before the softmax: lower is more deterministic, higher is more varied. Pretraining
    Tensor and pipeline parallelism
    Splitting the matrices inside each layer across GPUs, or splitting the layers into stages across GPUs. Fine-tuning
    TF-IDF
    Term weights that rise with a word's frequency in a document and fall with its frequency across documents. Sequence models
    Tokenization
    Splitting text into the model's vocabulary units, usually subwords learned with BPE or a similar method. Transformer
    Tool calling
    The model emits a structured request to call a function described by a schema; the application runs it and returns the result to the model. Tool calling
    Top-k and top-p sampling
    Sampling only from the \(k\) most likely tokens, or from the smallest set of tokens whose probabilities add up to \(p\). Pretraining
    Training–serving skew
    Differences between how features are computed in training and in production, which silently degrade a deployed model. MLOps
    Transfer learning
    Reusing a model trained on one task or dataset as the starting point for another. Pretraining
    Transformer
    An architecture built from self-attention and feed-forward blocks, with residual connections and normalization. Transformer
    TTFT (time to first token)
    Queueing time plus prefill time: how long a user waits before output starts streaming. Inference

    U¶

    Underfitting
    A model too simple to capture the signal, with high error on both training and validation data. ML fundamentals

    V¶

    VAE (variational autoencoder)
    An encoder outputs a distribution over latent codes and a decoder reconstructs the input; trained on the ELBO. Generative models
    Vanishing gradients
    Gradients shrinking exponentially through many layers or time steps, so early layers stop learning. Neural networks
    Vector database
    A store that indexes embeddings for fast similarity search, with metadata filters and updates. Embeddings
    Vision Transformer (ViT)
    A Transformer applied to a sequence of image patches. CNNs & vision

    W¶

    Warmup
    Raising the learning rate gradually over the first steps of training, for stability. Training
    Weight decay
    Shrinking weights slightly at every step. Equivalent to L2 regularization for SGD but not for Adam, hence AdamW. Training
    Word2vec
    Early word embeddings learned by predicting a word from its neighbors or the neighbors from the word (CBOW and skip-gram). Sequence models
    Workflow (LLM)
    A fixed sequence of LLM calls and code (chaining, routing, parallelization, orchestrator–workers), as opposed to an agent that chooses its own path. AI agents

    X–Z¶

    XGBoost
    A gradient-boosting library that uses second-order gradients, regularized leaf weights and sparsity-aware splits. Trees & ensembles
    Zero-shot
    Doing a task from instructions alone, with no examples. Prompting
