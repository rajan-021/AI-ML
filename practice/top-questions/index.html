    Most-Asked Questions¶

    How to use this page

    Each row is a question that comes up again and again, with the one-line core of a good answer. Cover the answer column, say your answer out loud, then compare. If your answer is weaker than the line, open the linked page; its interview section has the full answer. For spaced practice with every question on the site, use the Flashcard Drill.

    Foundations¶

    Question Core of the answer Page
    Explain the bias–variance trade-off. Error = bias² + variance + noise; simple models underfit, flexible ones overfit; diagnose with train vs validation error. ML Fundamentals
    L1 vs L2: why is L1 sparse? The L1 ball has corners on the axes, and its gradient doesn't shrink near zero, so weights hit exactly zero. ML Fundamentals
    What is data leakage? Examples? Information unavailable at prediction time reaching training: preprocessing before splitting, future data, target-derived features, duplicate users. ML Fundamentals
    How do you cross-validate time series? Forward-chaining splits: train on the past, validate on the next period; never shuffle. ML Fundamentals
    How do you handle imbalanced data? Right metric (PR-AUC, recall at fixed FPR), class weights or resampling on the training fold only, threshold tuning, recalibration. ML Fundamentals
    MLE vs MAP; priors and regularization? MAP adds a log prior; Gaussian prior = L2, Laplace prior = L1. Math for ML
    Why cross-entropy rather than MSE for classification? It's the correct likelihood, convex for logistic regression, and its gradient \(p - y\) doesn't vanish when confidently wrong. Math for ML
    What is a p-value? P(data at least this extreme given the null is true); not P(null is true). Math for ML
    Precision vs recall: when does each matter? Recall when misses are costly (screening, fraud); precision when false alarms are costly (spam, blocking). Evaluation
    What does ROC-AUC mean; when is it misleading? P(random positive scored above random negative); misleading under heavy imbalance, so use PR-AUC. Evaluation
    What is calibration and how do you fix it? Predicted 0.8 should mean 80% positive; fix with Platt scaling, isotonic regression or temperature scaling. Evaluation
    Explain NDCG. Position-discounted graded relevance, \(\sum rel_i/\log_2(i+1)\), divided by the ideal ordering's DCG. Evaluation
    How do you size an A/B test? \(n \approx 16\,p(1-p)/\delta^2\) per group for α = 0.05 and power 0.8; run whole weeks; don't peek. Evaluation
    Offline metric up, online metric down: why? Metric misaligned with the goal, leakage, training–serving skew, logged-data bias, latency, novelty, broken test. Evaluation

    Classical ML¶

    Question Core of the answer Page
    Assumptions of linear regression? Linearity, independent errors, constant variance, roughly normal errors (for inference), no perfect multicollinearity. Linear models
    Interpret a logistic regression coefficient. Change in log-odds per unit; \(e^w\) is the odds ratio, not a probability multiplier. Linear models
    Why not MSE for logistic regression? Non-convex through the sigmoid and vanishing gradients when wrong; log loss is convex and correct. Linear models
    What are support vectors and the kernel trick? Points on or inside the margin define the boundary; kernels compute dot products in a feature space without building it. kNN, NB & SVM
    Why is Naive Bayes "naive", and why does it work? Assumes conditional independence; probabilities are off but class rankings often survive; great with little data. kNN, NB & SVM
    Bagging vs boosting? Bagging averages independent deep trees to cut variance; boosting adds shallow trees sequentially to cut bias. Trees & ensembles
    Why does a random forest sample features? To de-correlate trees: averaging only cancels variance that isn't shared. Trees & ensembles
    What makes XGBoost better than plain gradient boosting? Second-order optimization, regularized leaves, sparsity-aware splits, subsampling, fast parallel systems work. Trees & ensembles
    Trees vs neural nets on tabular data? GBDTs usually win on heterogeneous, medium-sized tabular data; nets win on raw text, images and huge ID spaces. Trees & ensembles
    How do you choose k in k-means? When does it fail? Elbow, silhouette, stability, usefulness; fails on non-round clusters, varied densities, outliers, unscaled features. Unsupervised
    Derive PCA; relation to SVD? Maximize projected variance → top eigenvectors of the covariance; the right singular vectors of centered data. Unsupervised
    Can you trust distances in t-SNE plots? No: cluster sizes and gaps are not meaningful; use for exploration only. Unsupervised
    Collaborative vs content-based filtering; cold start? Interactions vs item features; cold start with content features, popularity, onboarding and exploration. Recommender systems
    Why candidate generation then ranking? Latency: cheap high-recall retrieval over millions, expensive precise ranking over hundreds. Recommender systems

    Deep learning¶

    Question Core of the answer Page
    Why non-linear activations? Without them stacked layers collapse into one linear map. Neural networks
    Derive backprop for two layers. \(\partial Z_2 = P - Y\); \(\partial W = A^\top \partial Z\); pass back \(\partial Z\,W^\top\); multiply by the activation derivative. Neural networks
    Vanishing gradients: cause and fixes? Products of small per-layer factors; ReLU, good init, normalization, residuals, gated RNNs, clipping for explosions. Neural networks
    Xavier vs He initialization? Keep activation variance constant: \(2/(n_{in}+n_{out})\) for tanh, \(2/n_{in}\) for ReLU. Neural networks
    Explain Adam; why AdamW? Momentum + per-parameter scaling + bias correction; AdamW decouples weight decay from the adaptive step. Training
    Why learning-rate warmup? Early Adam statistics and random weights make big steps unstable, especially in Transformers. Training
    BatchNorm vs LayerNorm? Batch statistics per channel vs per-token feature statistics; LayerNorm doesn't depend on batch, so Transformers use it. Training
    Dropout at train vs test time? Randomly zero and rescale during training; off at inference. Training
    How do you debug a model that won't train? Check initial loss ≈ ln K, overfit one batch, inspect data and labels, check gradients and learning rate. Training
    Conv output size and parameters? \(\lfloor (W - k + 2p)/s \rfloor + 1\); parameters \((k^2 C_{in} + 1) C_{out}\). CNNs & vision
    How does ResNet solve degradation? Learn residuals \(F(x) + x\); identity is easy and gradients flow through skips. CNNs & vision
    ViT vs CNN? Patches as tokens, weak inductive bias, needs more data, scales better; CNNs are data-efficient. CNNs & vision
    How does CLIP do zero-shot classification? Compare the image embedding with text embeddings of "a photo of a {label}". CNNs & vision
    Why do RNNs struggle? Vanishing gradients, no parallelism over time, a fixed-size state bottleneck. Sequence models
    Walk through the LSTM gates. Forget, input, candidate, output; additive cell update keeps gradients alive. Sequence models
    How did attention fix seq2seq? The decoder takes a relevance-weighted mix of all encoder states at each step. Sequence models

    Transformers and LLMs¶

    Question Core of the answer Page
    Explain Q, K, V. Query = what I'm looking for, key = what I offer to match, value = what I pass on; weights from softmax of \(QK^\top/\sqrt{d_k}\). Transformer
    Why divide by √d_k? Dot products grow with dimension; large scores saturate softmax and kill gradients. Transformer
    Why multi-head attention? Several relationships at once at the same total cost. Transformer
    Why positional encodings? Explain RoPE. Attention is order-blind; RoPE rotates Q and K by position so scores depend on relative distance. Transformer
    Why are most LLMs decoder-only? One scalable objective over all text, every task as generation, efficient KV-cached inference. Transformer
    Parameters of a 32-layer, d = 4096 model? ≈ \(12d^2 \times 32 \approx 6.4\)B plus embeddings ≈ 6.7B. Transformer
    What is the KV cache; how big is it? Stored past keys and values; \(2 \times L \times h_{kv} \times d_h \times T \times\) bytes per sequence. Efficient attention
    MHA vs MQA vs GQA? Shared keys and values across heads shrink the cache; GQA keeps near-MHA quality. Efficient attention
    How does FlashAttention work? Exact attention in SRAM tiles with an online softmax; never writes the n×n matrix. Efficient attention
    How does MoE work; total vs active parameters? A router sends each token to top-k experts; memory follows total, compute follows active. Efficient attention
    BERT vs GPT objectives? Masked tokens with bidirectional context vs next token with causal context. Pretraining
    What did Chinchilla show? Scale data with parameters (~20 tokens per parameter for compute-optimal training). Pretraining
    Top-k vs top-p; temperature? Fixed candidate count vs probability mass; temperature divides logits to sharpen or flatten. Pretraining
    Why do LLMs hallucinate; how to reduce it? Trained for plausibility, not truth; ground with RAG and citations, allow abstaining, verify, evaluate. Pretraining
    Explain LoRA; why B = 0 at init? Learn low-rank \(\Delta W = BA\) on frozen weights; zero B starts exactly at the base model; merges with no latency. Fine-tuning
    Memory to fully fine-tune 7B? ≈ 16 bytes per parameter with Adam in mixed precision ≈ 112 GB plus activations. Fine-tuning
    Quantization: FP16 vs INT4 for 70B? ≈ 140 GB vs ≈ 35 GB of weights; PTQ methods such as GPTQ and AWQ. Fine-tuning
    DDP vs FSDP; tensor vs pipeline parallelism? Replicate vs shard training state; split matrices within layers vs split layers into stages. Fine-tuning
    Prefill vs decode? Prompt processed in parallel (compute-bound, TTFT) vs one token at a time (memory-bound, per-token latency). Inference
    Continuous batching and PagedAttention? Swap requests in and out every step; store the KV cache in pages to avoid waste. Inference
    How does speculative decoding preserve quality? A draft proposes tokens; the target verifies with rejection sampling, so outputs follow the target distribution exactly. Inference
    VAE vs GAN vs diffusion? Stable but blurry vs sharp but unstable vs high quality and stable but slow to sample. Generative models
    What is classifier-free guidance? Extrapolate from unconditional toward conditional noise predictions with scale \(w\). Generative models

    Alignment and reasoning¶

    Question Core of the answer Page
    Walk through RLHF. SFT → reward model from preference pairs (Bradley–Terry) → PPO maximizing reward minus KL to the reference. Post-training
    Why the KL penalty? The reward model is an imperfect proxy; KL limits reward hacking and drift. Post-training
    DPO vs PPO? DPO optimizes preferences directly with a classification-style loss, no reward model or sampling; PPO is on-policy and handles arbitrary rewards. Post-training
    What is Constitutional AI / RLAIF? AI feedback guided by written principles replaces much human labeling. Post-training
    What is reward hacking? Maximizing the proxy reward without the intended behavior: length, flattery, special-casing tests. Post-training
    Explain PPO's clipping. Clip the new/old probability ratio to \([1-\epsilon, 1+\epsilon]\) so updates stay small. RL essentials
    What is GRPO? PPO-style updates with group-normalized rewards as advantages; no critic. Reasoning
    What is test-time compute scaling? Better answers by spending more inference compute: longer reasoning, best-of-N with verifiers, search. Reasoning
    PRM vs ORM? Step-level rewards vs final-answer rewards. Reasoning

    AI engineering and agents¶

    Question Core of the answer Page
    How do you get reliably parseable output? Structured outputs or tool schemas with constrained decoding, then validate and retry. Prompting
    Bi-encoder vs cross-encoder? Separate embeddings for fast retrieval vs joint scoring for accurate reranking. Embeddings
    How does HNSW work; IVF vs HNSW? Layered proximity graph with greedy search; IVF clusters and is compact; HNSW has better recall and more memory. Embeddings
    Walk through a RAG pipeline. Parse → chunk → embed + BM25 → hybrid retrieval with ACL filters → rerank → prompt with citations → evaluate. RAG
    How do you choose a chunk size? Start from structure at a few hundred tokens with overlap; tune on retrieval recall with real questions. RAG
    Why hybrid search and reranking? Dense misses exact terms, BM25 misses meaning; rerankers add precision on a shortlist. RAG
    How do you evaluate RAG? Retrieval recall@k and MRR; faithfulness and correctness of answers; end-to-end success. RAG
    RAG vs fine-tuning vs long context? Knowledge vs behavior vs small stable corpora; combine them. RAG
    How does function calling work? Tools described by schema; the model emits a structured call; your code executes and returns the result. Tool calling
    How do you design good tools? Few, well-scoped, clearly described, typed, concise results, helpful errors, user-scoped permissions. Tool calling
    Workflow vs agent? Fixed code paths vs the model choosing the path; use agents only when steps can't be known upfront. Agents
    Explain ReAct. Alternate reasoning, actions and observations in a loop. Agents
    Why do agents fail on long tasks? Errors compound (\(0.95^{20} \approx 0.36\)), context fills, goals drift; verify, checkpoint, compact. Agents
    What problem does MCP solve? N × M integrations become N + M with a standard protocol for tools, resources and prompts. MCP & A2A
    Host vs client vs server; tools vs resources vs prompts? App, per-server connector, capability provider; model-, app- and user-controlled primitives. MCP & A2A
    MCP vs A2A? Agent-to-tools/data vs agent-to-agent with tasks and artifacts. MCP & A2A
    What is context engineering? Curating the smallest high-signal context at each step: just-in-time retrieval, compaction, notes, sub-agents. Context engineering
    What is an agent harness? The loop, tools, sandbox, permissions, context, verification, state, tracing and evals around the model. Harness engineering
    pass@k vs pass^k? At least one success in k tries (capability) vs all k succeed (reliability). Harness engineering
    Pros and cons of LLM-as-judge? Scales subjective grading; biased by position, length and self-preference; calibrate against humans. LLM evals
    Direct vs indirect prompt injection; defenses? User-typed vs hidden in content; limit capabilities, approvals, isolate untrusted content, restrict egress. Security

    Production and design¶

    Question Core of the answer Page
    Notebook to production? Reproducible pipeline, registry, packaging, shadow → canary → A/B, monitoring, rollback, retraining. MLOps
    Training–serving skew? Features computed differently offline and online; shared definitions, point-in-time joins, logging served features. MLOps
    Covariate shift vs concept drift? \(P(x)\) changes vs \(P(y \mid x)\) changes; detect with distribution tests and delayed labels. MLOps
    ML system design framework? Clarify, frame, data, features, model, training, evaluation, serving and monitoring. ML system design
    Position bias and delayed labels? Model position and correct with propensities; train on matured labels, use proxies, monitor leading indicators. ML system design
    Design an enterprise RAG assistant. Connectors, parsing, chunking, hybrid index with ACLs, rerank, cite or abstain, eval set, monitoring, cost. GenAI system design
    SHAP vs LIME? Shapley-value attributions with consistency guarantees vs local linear surrogates. Responsible AI
    Two fairness metrics that conflict? Demographic parity vs equalized odds (or calibration) when base rates differ. Responsible AI
